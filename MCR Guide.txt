PART 1: MORTGAGE 101 FOR DEVELOPERS 
Context: Before writing code, you must understand the business entities you are counting. 
1.1 Key Terminology 
● The "Pipeline": This refers to all loans currently "in progress." They have applied but 
haven't finished the process. Think of this like a "Shopping Cart" that hasn't checked out 
yet. 
● "Closed/Funded": A loan that is finished. The borrower got the money. This is a 
"Completed Order." 
● "Lien Position": The priority of the debt. 
○ 1st Lien: The primary mortgage. If the house is sold, this gets paid first. 
○ 2nd Lien (Subordinate): A smaller loan (like a HELOC) behind the first one. 
● "Points" vs. "Fees": 
○ Origination Fee: A service charge the broker charges to do the work. 
○ Discount Points: A fee the borrower pays to "buy down" their interest rate to a lower 
%. 
1.2 The "Snapshot" Problem (Time-Travel Logic) 
● The Critical Challenge: Regulatory reports are based on Historical Status, not current 
status. 
● The Scenario: A loan is "Funded" today (e.g., April 15). However, if the user runs a report 
for Q1 (Jan 1 – Mar 31), the loan had not funded as of March 31. 
● The Rule: In the Q1 report, this loan must appear as "Processing" (Pipeline), NOT 
"Funded." 
● The Requirement: You cannot query the live current_status column in the database. You 
must query the LoanStatusHistory log. 
● Query Logic: "Select the status of Loan ID #X as of [Report End Date] 23:59:59." 
PART 2: CORE ARCHITECTURE & LOGIC

PART 5: ADVANCED LOGIC & DEFINITIONS 
5.1 Logic: Amount, Count, Average 
For every row in the report, calculate these three values: 
1. Amount ($): SUM(Loan Amount) for all matching loans. 
2. Count (#): COUNT(Unique Loan IDs) for all matching loans. 
3. Average ($): Amount / Count. 
○ Validation: If Count is 0, display 0 (prevent divide-by-zero errors). 
5.2 NMLS Definitions (Glossary) 
● Application: Oral or written request for extension of credit. 
● Closed: Loans that have funded and have legally binding agreements. 
● Broker Fee: Any fee collected in conjunction with brokering a loan (Origination, 
Processing). 
● Lender Fee: Fees collected for closing/funding (Admin, Underwriting). 
PART 6: VALIDATION & QA CHECKLIST 
Before releasing the module, run these mandatory checks: 
1. Pipeline Balance Check: 
○ Formula: (Start Pipeline) + (Received) - (Closed + Denied + Withdrawn) = (End 
Pipeline) 
○ Verification: Does AC066 (Calculated Pipeline Total) exactly match AC090 (Pipeline 
Results)? 
2. Closed Loan Vertical Check: 
○ Verification: Does AC070 (Total Closed Count) match the total count in AC190 (Total 
by Type)? 
○ Verification: Does AC070 match AC290 (Total by Property)? 
○ Verification: Does AC070 match AC390 (Total by Purpose)? 
○ Verification: Does AC070 match AC590 (Total by Lien)? 
3. Revenue Zero-Sum Check: 
○ Verification: Does the sum of all MLO Volume (Tab 4) equal the Company Total (Tab 2, 
AC070)? 
4. Status History Check: 
○ Test: Find a loan funded after the quarter end date. 
○ Verification: Ensure it appears as "Active/Processing" in the report, NOT "Funded."
